One continuous fight, the two fighters always in frame and always in violent motion, fast real-time combat, no slow motion, no posing, handheld camera following the action with motion blur, sparks, fire, blue wind and stone debris flying. Same arena, same night lighting, same two characters: the silver-armored knight with blue wind wings and a long sword, and the blue-skinned horned witch with a long dark needle-dagger who throws red fire. The knight slashes at the witch, the witch ducks under the blade and blasts a torrent of red fire point blank, the knight blocks it with her sword and is shoved backwards sliding on the stone, then she plants her feet and charges forward through the fire.
