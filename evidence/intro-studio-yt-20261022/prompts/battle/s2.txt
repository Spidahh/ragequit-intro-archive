One continuous fight, the two fighters always in frame and always in violent motion, fast real-time combat, no slow motion, no posing, handheld camera following the action with motion blur, sparks, fire, blue wind and stone debris flying. Same arena, same night lighting, same two characters: the silver-armored knight with blue wind wings and a long sword, and the blue-skinned horned witch with a long dark needle-dagger who throws red fire. The knight bursts out of the fire and strikes, a blast of blue wind hurls the witch backwards into a stone pillar that cracks and crumbles, the witch springs back up and slams her hand on the floor sending a wave of fire racing at the knight, who leaps high over it beating her wings.
