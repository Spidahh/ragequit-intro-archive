One continuous fight, the two fighters always in frame and always in violent motion, fast real-time combat, no slow motion, no posing, handheld camera following the action with motion blur, sparks, fire, blue wind and stone debris flying. Same arena, same night lighting, same two characters: the silver-armored knight with blue wind wings and a long sword, and the blue-skinned horned witch with a long dark needle-dagger who throws red fire. The knight dives down sword first from the air, the witch thrusts a lance of fire upward, they collide in an explosion of light and are thrown apart, then they crash together again, sword and needle-dagger grinding against each other face to face with sprays of sparks.
